Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

Welcome to RewardPulse. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us while participating in our consumer reward and feedback promotions.

1. Information We Collect

When interacting with our promotional portals, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact data: Primary email address, first name, zip code, and demographic details.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and approximate geographical location.
  • Survey responses: Voluntary feedback on consumer products, services, and shopping preferences.

2. How Your Information Is Used

We use collected data solely to:

  • Determine regional eligibility for active reward tiers and partner surveys.
  • Systematically dispatch digital reward credentials and confirmation tokens to your inbox.
  • Prevent multiple unauthorized entries and enforce anti-bot protection.

3. Third-Party Research Sponsors

RewardPulse works alongside accredited third-party performance research networks. When choosing to fulfill a sponsor questionnaire, you may be redirected to an encrypted third-party partner page subject to that specific partner's independent privacy guidelines.

4. Data Protection & Security

All communication with our servers is secured via 256-bit SSL encryption. We do not sell your personally identifiable data to unverified telemarketing networks.
